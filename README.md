# Hi, I'm Diego 👋

Engineer and pilgrim from Zaragoza, Spain. By day I co-lead Tech Enablement at the Global AI Office of [NTT DATA](https://www.nttdata.com/global/en/) GIH/HQ, where I help teams put applied AI to real use. The rest of the time you'll find me walking the Camino de Santiago, playing with small robots, or building something my family or I really need.

I'm Gen X. I went back to writing code when AI tools let me go from an idea to something that works in one evening. My book is about that change, and so are most of these repos.

Most of what I build ends up on [diegoromero.es](https://diegoromero.es/en/), where you can try it without installing anything.

## Things you can try

- [LLM Benchmarks Explorer](https://llm-benchmarks.diegoromero.es/): the Artificial Analysis leaderboard with filters for intelligence, price, speed and latency. Code: [aa-llm-benchmarking](https://github.com/dromerosm/aa-llm-benchmarking).
- [Geospatial Intelligence Platform](https://geospatial-platform.diegoromero.es): wildfire early warning for Aragón, built on public data (NASA FIRMS, AEMET, EFFIS). A work in progress. Code: [geospatial-intelligent-platform](https://github.com/dromerosm/geospatial-intelligent-platform).
- [Carta](https://carta.diegoromero.es/): turn OpenStreetMap streets into a print of your city. Code: [carta-maps](https://github.com/dromerosm/carta-maps).
- [Tiny Robotics](https://robotics.diegoromero.es/): my lab for two small M5Stack robots, a StackChan and a self-balancing BALA2. It has an ESP32 emulator that runs in your browser. Code: [tiny-robotics-bots](https://github.com/dromerosm/tiny-robotics-bots).
- More demos on the [prototypes page](https://diegoromero.es/en/prototypes/) and on [Hugging Face](https://huggingface.co/dromerosm).

## Research I've published

- [fm-smishing-bench](https://github.com/dromerosm/fm-smishing-bench): can Apple's on-device Foundation Models spot an SMS scam? A reproducible benchmark against gpt-oss.
- [teaching-one-decision](https://github.com/dromerosm/teaching-one-decision): code and data from a study on teaching one business decision (email triage) to small and large models.
- [openalex-semsearch-cli](https://github.com/dromerosm/openalex-semsearch-cli): semantic paper search and impact metrics on OpenAlex, from the terminal.
